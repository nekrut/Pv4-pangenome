PvP01 — GCA_900093555.2

This assembly is one of eight Plasmodium vivax strains in the BRC pangenome track hub. The strain is PvP01; the sequence is GenBank assembly GCA_900093555.2. The default view opens on the dihydropteroate synthase (dhps) locus, the sulfadoxine-resistance landmark used to anchor the hub.
