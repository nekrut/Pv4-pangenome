HyPhy BUSTED selection scans

These tracks report episodic diversifying selection per gene across the eight Plasmodium vivax strains. Each gene's coding sequence is tested with BUSTED — the Branch-Site Unrestricted Statistical Test for Episodic Diversification from the HyPhy package — and scored by its q-value (the false-discovery-rate-adjusted significance of selection). Two sets are shown, differing only in how complete a gene must be across strains to enter the test: strict core orthogroups (1,584 genes; present and intact in at least seven strains) and relaxed core orthogroups (4,215 genes; at least five strains).

Display Conventions and Configuration

Each feature is a gene in BED12, colored and scored by its BUSTED q-value, so the strongest signals of selection stand out. The strongest hit, Pvs230, also illustrates the multiple-hits correction — re-running it under BUSTED-MH, which allows two- and three-nucleotide substitutions, shifts its p-value and guards against false positives driven by clustered changes.

Methods

We extracted coding sequence for each strain with gffread, built eight-way codon alignments with MAFFT followed by codon back-translation against the protein alignment, and trimmed them with trimAl in automated1 mode. We inferred a per-gene tree with IQ-TREE under model selection (-m MFP) and ran BUSTED from HyPhy 2.5.99 on each alignment. Exact tool versions and parameters are recorded in the pipeline repository.
