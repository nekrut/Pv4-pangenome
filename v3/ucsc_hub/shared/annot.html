Gene projections

This composite shows gene models on the current strain. One subtrack carries the strain's own annotation; the others carry genes projected onto it from the curated anchor strains, so a gene present in one strain can be located in every other strain even where no native annotation exists.

Display Conventions and Configuration

Models are drawn in BED12 — thick blocks are coding exons, thin blocks are untranslated regions, and the connecting line carries strand arrows. Each subtrack is labeled by its source: "self" for the strain's native annotation, "annot from <anchor>" for a projection. The self-annotation is enabled by default; projection subtracks are hidden until selected.

Methods

Native annotations come from PlasmoDB release 68 for the curated strains and from the NCBI assembly records for the rest. We projected each anchor's genes onto every other strain in two passes — Liftoff for the straightforward liftovers, then TOGA2 with CESAR to rescue the genes Liftoff could not place cleanly across the larger rearrangements. Exact tool versions and parameters are recorded in the pipeline repository.
